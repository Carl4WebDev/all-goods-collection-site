Got questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Will a jacket count in the 3-for-₱999 deal?

Our recent posts say all items. Confirm with us when you order, since promos can change.

How do I check the fit?

Ask for chest width, length and sleeve length. Measure a jacket you own the same way and compare.

Is the jacket waterproof?

Not every jacket is water-resistant. Ask us to check the tag and fabric before you buy.

Can I see the inside lining?

Yes. Ask for photos of the lining, zipper and cuffs so you can check the condition.
