Fit check

What Makes a Good Fit for Your Style?

Japan-surplus pieces can be sized differently from local brands, and each item is one of a kind. Before you buy, compare the measurements we send with a piece you already own. That is the easiest way to know it will fit.

Got questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I reserve an item before paying?

Message us right away with the item photo. Ask how long it can be held and when payment is needed, since most pieces are one of a kind.

Can someone else buy for me?

Yes, a friend or family member can order for you. Make sure you agree on the size, the item and who will pick it up.

How fast do items sell?

Popular pieces can go within a day of posting. Turn on notifications for our Facebook page so you see new drops early.

What should I ask before paying?

Ask for the final total, the exact items in your bundle, any flaws and how pickup works. Keep the GCash receipt and the chat for reference.
